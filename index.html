About

Building growth where retail, people and measurement meet.

Mike O'Farrell is a retail executive with more than a decade of leadership experience spanning travel retail, omnichannel commerce, strategic accounts and sales growth.

After building and leading major retail businesses at Bose, Mike joined EssilorLuxottica as Director, Sales. He is also building Omni Retail Gravity, an independent approach to understanding and proving the influence of physical retail.

His work and writing focus on a simple question: how do we measure what actually changes consumer behavior, not just what is easiest to track?
