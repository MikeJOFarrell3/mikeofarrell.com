Real Retail #1

The Truth About Commerce in America

Physical retail isn't dying. The larger problem is that the systems used to measure commerce can make physical influence less visible than digital activity.

The first Real Retail essay examines the mismatch between where consumers actually shop and where measurement, attention and investment tend to flow.

Digital commerce produces abundant trackable signals. Physical retail is harder to attribute with the same precision, even when a store visit, product interaction or conversation plays an important role in a purchase decision.

That creates a strategic risk: businesses can begin funding what they can measure rather than what most strongly changes customer behavior.

Real Retail is an ongoing exploration of that measurement gap and what better commercial decision-making could look like.
